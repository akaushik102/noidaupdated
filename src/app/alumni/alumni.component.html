An Alumni Network That Spans The World

Over two decades, Amity University Noida has built a graduate community that reaches well beyond its campus in Sector 125. The Amity alumni association today connects professionals holding leadership positions at globally recognised organisations, founders running their own companies, researchers at international institutions, and mentors who continue to shape the careers of students coming after them.

Understanding the importance of alumni network for career growth, the Amity Alumni Association was built with a clear purpose: to keep graduates connected to each other, to current students, and to new opportunities throughout their professional lives.

Amity’s alumni network spans industries including technology, banking and finance, consulting, healthcare, law, media, manufacturing, and entrepreneurship. Geographically, alumni are based across India and in countries including the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Germany, Singapore, Malaysia, Japan, the UAE, and beyond.

Your Alumni Network Is Your Longest-Running Career Asset

The benefits of the Amity alumni network are not limited to the years spent on campus. For current students, alumni engagement directly shapes career outcomes. For graduates, the network offers professional resources, peer connections, and a lifelong relationship with the institution.

For Current Students

Alumni mentorship for career growth is embedded into the academic calendar at Amity. Students benefit from direct access to working professionals who have navigated the same programs, the same placement drives, and the same early-career decisions they are now facing.

Specific benefits include career guidance from the alumni network through structured counselling sessions, alumni-led internship and placement guidance, industry exposure through knowledge sharing sessions and panel discussions, and access to alumni who have built careers at companies like Siemens, Intel, Oracle, Kantar, and Standard Chartered.

For Alumni

The alumni membership benefits extend well beyond graduation. As a member of the Amity Alumni Association, graduates can access the alumni portal at amiconnect.amity.edu, connect with 200,000+ professionals worldwide, explore job postings and internship opportunities shared through the network, and attend alumni events and reunions on campus and internationally.

Additional professional advantages of
alumni association membership include:

  • Access to AU-BSI professional development courses at subsidised rates, with 20% discount on all BSI training programs
  • Entrepreneurship mentoring support through the Amity Centre for Entrepreneurship Development (ACED)
  • Guidance and counselling services through ACGC, available 24x7 on a toll-free number
  • Innovation and startup support via the Amity Innovation and Design Centre (AIDC)
  • Continued engagement as a speaker, mentor, jury member, or industry collaborator
