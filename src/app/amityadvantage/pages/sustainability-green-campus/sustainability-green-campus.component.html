Sustainability & Green Campus

Amity University Noida is committed to sustainability and eco-conscious living. Our green campus initiatives make us one of the most environmentally responsible universities in India.

From renewable energy adoption to waste recycling and rainwater harvesting, sustainability is deeply embedded in the campus ecosystem. Students are actively involved in green projects, ensuring that sustainability is not just taught but practiced.

With tree plantation drives, eco-friendly transportation, and clean energy initiatives, Amity is preparing students to become responsible citizens of tomorrow.
