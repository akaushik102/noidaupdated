Cultural Diversity & Vibrant Community

Amity University Noida is a melting pot of cultures, ideas, and traditions. With students from across India and over 100 countries, our campus reflects true global diversity.

The university celebrates inclusivity through cultural events, festivals, and student-run clubs that promote collaboration and leadership. Whether it’s celebrating Diwali, Holi, Christmas, or international student festivals, Amity is always alive with energy.

Dedicated support services for international students ensure that they feel at home, making Amity one of the most preferred destinations for global learners.

Amity Noida isn’t just a university – it’s a global community where every culture finds a home.
