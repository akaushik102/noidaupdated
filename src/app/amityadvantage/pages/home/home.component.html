Top Recruiters

Amity University Noida is consistently recognized by Times Higher Education Rankings as a top global university for producing highly employable graduates. This success isn't accidental, it's driven by our Corporate Resource Centre (CRC), which offers robust, 100% placement support to every student. The CRC is dedicated to bridging the industry-academia gap through targeted initiatives: securing industry exposure, facilitating internship opportunities, and providing rigorous support including mock interviews, personality enhancement, and essential skill development.
