Career Development & Placements

One of Amity University Noida’s biggest strengths is its outstanding placement record. With over 1,050 recruiters and 11,000+ job offers in 2023 alone, Amity is among India’s most successful universities for placements. Companies like Microsoft, Amazon, Deloitte, Accenture, IBM, and Flipkart regularly recruit from our campus.

The dedicated Career Resource Centre (CRC) trains students through workshops, mock interviews, aptitude tests, and soft skills development programs, ensuring they are ready to grab top opportunities. In 2025, B.Tech students secured packages up to INR 35.9 LPA, with an average of INR 9.6 LPA.

Beyond placements, Amity’s alumni network provides mentorship, guidance, and global networking opportunities that help students throughout their careers.

Choose Amity Noida for placements that open doors to your dream career.
