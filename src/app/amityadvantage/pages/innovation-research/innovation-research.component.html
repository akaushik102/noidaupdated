Innovation & Research

Innovation is in Amity University Noida’s DNA. With a strong emphasis on research and entrepreneurship, students are encouraged to think beyond conventional boundaries and create impactful solutions.

Our dedicated research centers focus on interdisciplinary studies, fostering collaboration between students and faculty across various fields. Amity also supports startups and entrepreneurial ventures through incubation centers, seed funding, and mentorship programs.

Hackathons and innovation challenges are a regular part of campus life, inspiring students to solve real-world problems with creativity and technology. Amity graduates are known not only for their knowledge but also for their ability to innovate.

At Amity, research and innovation aren’t side projects – they’re part of everyday learning.
