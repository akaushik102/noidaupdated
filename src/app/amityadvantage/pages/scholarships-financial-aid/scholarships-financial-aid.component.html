Inclusive Scholarships & Financial Aid

At Amity University Noida, we believe talent should never be held back by financial constraints. That’s why we offer one of India’s most comprehensive scholarship and financial aid programs.

From merit-based scholarships for high achievers to need-based support for deserving students, Amity ensures that everyone has access to quality education. Special provisions are made for differently-abled students to create an inclusive learning environment.

Scholarships also extend to students excelling in sports, arts, and other fields, reflecting Amity’s commitment to nurturing diverse talent.

At Amity, we don’t just invest in education – we invest in your dreams.
