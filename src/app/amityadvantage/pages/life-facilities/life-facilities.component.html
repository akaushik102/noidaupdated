Residential Life & Facilities

Life at Amity University Noida is more than just academics – it’s a vibrant residential experience where students build friendships, networks, and memories for life.

Our hostels are designed with comfort and safety in mind, offering modern amenities, Wi-Fi, and 24/7 security. Dining facilities cater to diverse tastes with hygienic, nutritious, and multi-cuisine options. Student lounges and recreational spaces provide areas to relax, collaborate, and recharge after a busy day of learning.

The residential community at Amity is inclusive and diverse, bringing together students from across India and the world in a safe, welcoming environment.

At Amity Noida, the campus is not just where you study – it’s where you live, learn, and grow.
