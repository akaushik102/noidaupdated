Sports & Extra-Curricular Excellence

At Amity University Noida, education goes hand-in-hand with sports, culture, and extracurricular activities. We believe in nurturing well-rounded individuals who excel in academics as well as in life.

The campus boasts world-class sports facilities, including stadiums, fitness centers, and arenas for cricket, football, basketball, tennis, and more. Students regularly participate in national and international tournaments, bringing glory to the university.

Cultural festivals, debates, theatre, and music events create an energetic campus life. Whether it’s the annual Amity Youth Fest or inter-college competitions, Amity ensures students have countless opportunities to showcase their talents.

Life at Amity is not just about classrooms – it’s about discovering your passions and excelling in them.
